> restaurant and contains no personal data. Metrics computed on it show that the pipelines run and how
> baselines compare on simulated patterns; they are **not** evidence of real-world performance.

## Generator parameters (all recorded in `manifest.json → config`)

- **Daily volume:** negative binomial (gamma–Poisson, shape 25) around `baseOrdersPerDay` (105 Cairo, 85 Riyadh)
  × weekday multiplier (Mon … Sun: 0.92, 0.93, 0.98, **1.22 Thu**, **1.30 Fri**, **1.15 Sat**, 0.90 — Friday–Saturday
  weekend in both countries) × 8 %/year growth × ±5 % annual seasonality × Ramadan 0.88 × Eid 1.35.
- **Meal peaks:** mixture of normals for the order hour — lunch 14:00 (σ 1.2 h, 40 %), dinner 21:00 (σ 1.5 h, 50 %),
  late night 00:36 (10 %). **Ramadan:** iftar 18:21 (σ 0.45 h, 55 %), evening 22:00 (27 %), suhoor 02:00 (18 %).
- **Fulfilment:** dine-in 45 % / pickup 20 % / delivery 35 %; Ramadan: 32 % / 18 % / 50 %.
- **Customers:** 58 % of orders are linked to a known customer; propensity ~ Gamma(0.9, 1); 35 % present at the start,
  others join uniformly; exponential lifetime with mean 320 days (latent churn); favourite main chosen with
  probability 0.5 (repeat purchases).
- **Baskets:** party size 1 + Poisson(0.7); per main: side 62 % (60 % the main's preferred side), drink 70 % (50 % the
  preferred drink); dessert 14 % (38 % in Ramadan, mostly qatayef/kunafa); sobia in 35 % of Ramadan drinks.
- **Prices:** EGP list prices; Riyadh uses 0.16 × EGP rounded to whole riyals.
- **Delivery drop-offs:** customer home (or random for anonymous orders) at Gamma(2.2, 1.4) km (mean ≈ 3.1 km, max 9 km) around the branch.

## Known limitations

- Effects are stylised and independent (no weather, promotions, stock-outs, price changes or staff effects).
- Ramadan/Eid dates are fixed approximations; real dates depend on moon sighting.
- Because volume is set top-down and customers are drawn proportionally to propensity, individual visit rates
  depend on how many customers are active that day.
- Planted structure (preferred sides/drinks, favourite dishes) makes recommendation baselines look good; real
  menus will be noisier.
